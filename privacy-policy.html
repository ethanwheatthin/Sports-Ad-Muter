Privacy Policy

S.A.M — Sports Ad Muter  •  Last updated: April 2026

Short version: S.A.M processes everything on your own computer. No data ever leaves your device. We collect nothing.

1. Overview

S.A.M (Sports Ad Muter) is a Chrome browser extension that automatically mutes advertisements during live sports streams. It works by capturing frames from the video you are watching and analyzing them using a locally installed AI model (Ollama) running entirely on your own machine.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

S.A.M does not collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share any of the following:

  • Video frames or screenshots captured from your browser
  • URLs or titles of pages you visit
  • Personal information of any kind
  • Usage statistics or analytics
  • Crash reports or telemetry
  • Browser history

3. Data Stored Locally

S.A.M stores the following data only on your own device using Chrome's built-in storage APIs (chrome.storage.sync and chrome.storage.local):

  • Extension settings — your Ollama API URL, selected AI model, check interval, sport mode, and system prompt. Stored in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may sync across your own signed-in devices using your Google Account's encryption.
  • Recent frame captures — the last few video frames analyzed, stored temporarily in chrome.storage.local for display in the extension popup. These are never sent anywhere and are cleared when you click "Clear" or reinstall the extension.

4. How Analysis Works

When S.A.M detects a video, it periodically captures a single frame and sends it to your locally running Ollama instance (default: http://localhost:11434). This request never leaves your computer. No third-party server, cloud service, or external API is involved. The AI model runs entirely on your own hardware.

5. Permissions Explained

  • activeTab — Required to interact with the video element on the page you are currently viewing.
  • storage — Required to save your settings and temporary frame data locally.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>) — Required so the extension can inject its content script on any website where you may be watching a live sports stream (e.g., ESPN, YouTube, Peacock, Paramount+, etc.).

6. Third-Party Services

S.A.M does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. The only external communication is to the Ollama API running on localhost, which is your own machine.

7. Children's Privacy

S.A.M does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the extension constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
